Our Journey

Started With a Simple Belief

In 2018, founders Elena and Marcus Hartwell noticed something: every home they visited had a generic, mass-produced doormat that said nothing about the people inside. So they started DoorMat Studio in their garage in Austin, Texas, with one mission — to make entrances as personal as the lives lived behind them.

Eight years later, with 12,000+ happy customers, a team of 18 dedicated artisans, and recognition from Houzz and Better Homes & Gardens, we remain as passionate about craftsmanship and personalization as we were on day one.

Our Journey

Eight Years of Growth & Impact

From a garage in Austin to a nationally recognized custom mat studio — here's how we grew.

2018 — Founded in Austin, TX

Elena and Marcus create their first 10 custom mats in the garage. Word spreads locally through Instagram.

2019 — First 500 Customers

Launched the online store and design tool. Reached 500 happy customers by December.

2020 — Pandemic Growth

As people nested at home, demand for personalized mats skyrocketed. Team grew to 8 artisans.

2021 — Houzz Award Winner

Recognized by Houzz as one of the top custom home accessory studios in North America.

2023 — B2B & Hotel Division

Launched the bulk orders and hotel mat program, serving 50+ hospitality brands nationwide.

2026 — 12,000+ Customers & Growing

Now with 18 team members, 500+ designs, and a 4.9-star average. The warmest mat studio in America.
