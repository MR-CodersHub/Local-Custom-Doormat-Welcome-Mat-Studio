About Natural Coir

Coir is the natural fiber extracted from the husk of coconuts. Harvested sustainably from the outer fibrous shell, it's been used for centuries to create durable, long-lasting mats. Our coir is sourced from certified eco-friendly plantations in South India and Sri Lanka.

The natural brown color of coir is its authentic, untreated hue. When dyes are applied for our custom designs, we use UV-resistant, water-based inks that are safe for family and pets.

Caring for Your Coir Mat

1
Regular Shaking

Pick up and shake the mat outdoors weekly to remove trapped dirt and debris from the bristles.

2
Vacuum Occasionally

Use a stiff brush attachment to vacuum the surface for deeper cleaning without damaging the fibers.

3
Spot Clean Spills

For spills, blot immediately with a dry cloth. Use mild soap and cold water for stains, then air dry flat.

4
Avoid Prolonged Soaking

Do not soak or machine wash. Bring indoors during heavy rain or flooding to extend mat life.

Care Tip: Natural coir mats may shed a small amount of fiber during the first few weeks of use — this is normal and will stop after initial settling.
